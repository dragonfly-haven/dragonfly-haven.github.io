A deep black dragonfly in tiny dashes, seen from straight above, drawn with as much of its wings' veins and cells as its size and the screen allow. As it first appears it beats its wings for a moment, on phones too, then keeps still until you hover over it, when it beats them as a hovering dragonfly does: hind and fore wings in counterstroke, sweeping down and forward, then up and back, slowed so the eye can follow.
